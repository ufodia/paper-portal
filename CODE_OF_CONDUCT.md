# Community conduct

Paper Portal welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities and technical experience.

Be considerate and specific. Discuss the game, evidence or implementation rather than attacking a person. Respect privacy, give credit and help newcomers understand the tools without dismissing their questions.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, repeated personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, limit participation or block accounts to protect the community. They should apply decisions consistently and explain them when doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for platform abuse. For a project-conduct concern, ask for a private contact route without publishing identifying details or sensitive evidence. This document does not promise a staffed or continuously monitored reporting channel.
